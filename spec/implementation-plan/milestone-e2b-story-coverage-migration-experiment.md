## WHAT REGRESSED (why I stopped)

### Regression 1 — false-insufficient on sufficiency (DANGEROUS-ERROR class, §15)
Coverage/2 systematically under-calls sufficiency vs the human reference AND the Coverage/1
baseline:

| Fixture | HR | Coverage/1 | Coverage/2 | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I_no_intervention_sufficient | **sufficient** | sufficient ✅ | **workable** | ❌ NEW false-insufficient (C1 was right) |
| J_anti_checklist_solitude | **sufficient** | sufficient ✅ | **workable** | ❌ NEW false-insufficient (C1 was right) |
| H3_accum | **sufficient** | workable | workable | ⚠️ wrong vs HR, but C1 also missed it (not NEW) |
| A (semantic-envelope) | workable OR sufficient | sufficient | workable | ✅ within envelope |
| all others | — | match | match | ✅ no change |

**2 NEW false-insufficient regressions (I, J)** where Coverage/1 was correct. Both are "enough"
Stories (I = no-intervention-sufficient, J = solitude already complete for its narrow intent)
that Coverage/2 downgraded to `workable`. False-insufficient is dangerous-error priority #3 and
the gate forbids a material regression.

### Regression 2 — Story-Idea concision NOT fixed + `could_use_more → idea` coupling (hypothesis #3 FAILED)
The architecture was expected to make ideas concise by responsibility separation. It did not:

| | Coverage/1 idea words | Coverage/2 idea words |
|---|---|---|
| range (ideas present) | 81–133 | 63–94 |
| B / H2 | 83 / 104 | **94 / 90** |

Ideas are modestly shorter but still **60–94-word paragraphs** — the SF dogfood bulk problem
is NOT solved. And a NEW coupling appeared: Coverage/2 emits an idea on **I and J (HR says
null)** because it treats every `could_use_more` dimension as an opportunity to surface. Giving
the model an explicit multi-dimension Guide actually *increased* its urge to find a gap per weak
dimension and justify it in prose — the opposite of the intended effect.

Root cause (diagnosed, not tuned): the Guide gives the model a richer scaffold of "dimensions
that could be stronger", so (a) it rarely calls a Story fully sufficient while any dimension is
< strong (→ false-insufficient), and (b) it always finds a weak dimension to write an idea
about (→ unnecessary ideas + length). This is a **prompt-responsibility** problem, not an
architecture-contradiction: the /2 prompt says sufficiency ≠ Guide completion and "ONE concise
idea or null", but the mere presence of the Guide biases the model toward completion-thinking.

- INSUFFICIENT: not enough suitable, complementary material to meaningfully satisfy intent at scope.
- WORKABLE: a coherent Story can be made now, but current material meaningfully CONSTRAINS the result

- SUFFICIENT: enough suitable, complementary material for a satisfying Story at scope without an
  important limitation from MISSING material (does NOT require no room to improve).
